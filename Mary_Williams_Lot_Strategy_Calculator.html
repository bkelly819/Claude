Important assumptions and limitations

  • This is an illustrative financial planning model. It is not a tax calculation and is not tax, legal or investment advice. Confirm all tax treatment with a qualified CPA or estate attorney.
  • Growth rates are user-entered planning assumptions. Neither the property nor the investment is guaranteed to earn any return, and actual results will differ.
  • The step-up in basis at death is an assumption based on current federal law (IRC §1014). Ownership structure, trusts, gifting during life, or future law changes can alter it.
  • Federal estate tax, New Jersey inheritance tax, and any other transfer taxes are not considered. Both strategies leave an asset in Mary's estate of roughly the value shown.
  • Property taxes on the lot are modeled using the entered amount and yearly increase, paid at the end of each year, and are not deducted for income tax purposes. Not modeled: selling costs and commissions, insurance, maintenance and other carrying costs of holding a vacant lot, income or capital gains taxes on investment earnings, inflation, and any tax the children would owe if they later sell.
  • Returns compound once a year. The sale is assumed to close today and the death date is assumed at the end of the final year.
